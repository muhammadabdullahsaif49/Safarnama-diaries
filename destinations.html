Mountain Escape

Hunza Valley

Surrounded by dramatic mountain peaks, Hunza is known for its beautiful valleys, historic forts, apricot orchards and breathtaking views.

  • Region: Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Best time: May to October
  • Highlights: Karimabad, Baltit Fort and mountain views

Plan Your Journey

When Should You Visit Pakistan?

Weather varies across Pakistan, from the northern mountains to the southern coast. Use these general seasonal suggestions when planning your trip.

01

Spring Adventures

Spring is a lovely time to explore many lower-altitude destinations, enjoy pleasant weather and see landscapes coming back to life.

02

Summer in the North

The warmer months are generally popular for exploring Hunza and Fairy Meadows. Check local road conditions and weather before travelling into the mountains.

03

Autumn and Winter Escapes

Autumn and winter can be suitable for exploring Lahore and coastal destinations such as Gwadar. Pack according to the local forecast.
