Our Story

More Than Just Destinations

Safarnama is an Urdu word for a travel story or travelogue. Safarnama Diaries is a travel website created to bring together experiences, photographs and useful information about beautiful places across Pakistan.

Travel is not only about reaching a destination. It is also about meeting people, discovering different cultures, tasting local food and making memories along the way.

Our website gives visitors a place to explore travel stories, discover destinations, learn practical travel tips and find inspiration for their next journey.

Developed as a web design course project, Safarnama Diaries combines travel-related content with a clean, accessible and responsive website design.

Behind the Website

About the Creator

Safarnama Diaries was developed as a web design course project. It demonstrates how HTML and CSS can be used to create a multi-page website with consistent styling and organised content.

The project focuses on page structure, navigation, responsive layouts, accessible content and an engaging presentation of travel information.
