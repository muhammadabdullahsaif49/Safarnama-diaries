Plan Your Adventure

Everything You Need Before You Travel

Whether you are exploring mountain valleys, historic cities or remote landscapes, use these practical tips to prepare for a better travel experience.

🗺️

Before You Go

  1. Choose your destination and check the best season to visit.
  2. Check the weather forecast and road conditions, especially in mountain areas.
  3. Book your accommodation in advance during holiday seasons.
  4. Share your travel plan with family or friends and keep your phone charged.
  5. Carry some cash because card payments and mobile networks may not work everywhere.
🎒

Packing Checklist

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Warm layers for cold mountain nights
  • Rain jacket or umbrella
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat
  • Power bank and a basic first-aid kit
  • Reusable water bottle
  • CNIC or ID card and copies of important documents
🤝

Respect Local Culture

  • Dress respectfully, especially in villages and at religious places.
  • Ask permission before photographing people.
  • Do not leave rubbish behind. Carry it until you find a bin.
  • Support local shops and restaurants whenever possible.
🛡️

Travel Safely

  • Check local travel advisories and road conditions before departure.
  • Keep emergency contact numbers and your accommodation details handy.
  • Avoid unfamiliar routes after dark, particularly in remote areas.
  • Follow local guidance and avoid travelling during dangerous weather.
  • Keep your important belongings secure and stay aware of your surroundings.
Choose Your Season

Best Time to Visit Pakistan

Use this seasonal guide to plan your next trip around Pakistan.

Suggested visiting seasons for destinations featured on our website.
Destination Province / Region Best Time to Visit
Hunza Valley Gilgit-Baltistan May to October
Swat Valley Khyber Pakhtunkhwa April to October
Lahore Walled City Punjab October to March
Fairy Meadows Gilgit-Baltistan June to September
Gwadar Balochistan October to March
Tharparkar (Thar Desert) Sindh October to March; landscapes may be greener after monsoon rains.
Travel reminder: Seasons are general suggestions, not guarantees. Weather, road access, and local conditions can change. Always check current conditions before travelling, particularly when visiting mountains or remote destinations.
